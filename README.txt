INDUSTRIAL TRAINING E-PORTFOLIO (SQQXK4966)

SITE STRUCTURE
- index.html — Landing page
- about.html — About Me and career profile
- academic.html — Academic Journey (final-year project)
- internship.html — Industrial training, daily reports, and reflection
- career.html — Opens the career section on About Me

HOW TO OPEN
1. In Terminal, from this folder, run: python3 server.py
2. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765/ in the browser.

You can also open index.html directly in Chrome. The local server is the reliable way to view the internship daily reports.
